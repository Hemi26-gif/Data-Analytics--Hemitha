# FreshMart Business Performance Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

The **FreshMart Business Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard project designed to analyze the business performance of a multi-location retail company.

The project focuses on understanding sales, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, store performance, and sales channels. It helps management identify business trends, compare performance, and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

FreshMart manages multiple stores, products, customers, and sales channels. However, analyzing these datasets separately makes it difficult to understand overall business performance.

High sales do not always mean high profit because discounts, costs, and returns can affect profitability.

This project brings key business metrics together in an interactive dashboard to help identify strong-performing areas and those requiring further investigation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, costs, and profitability.
- Compare performance across stores and regions.
- Identify top- and low-performing products and categories.
- Understand customer and membership-segment contributions.
- Analyze discounts, returns, and sales channels.
- Present business insights through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The project uses six related datasets provided for the FreshMart analysis.

| Dataset | Records | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| FreshMart_Customers | 700 | Customer demographics and membership |
| FreshMart_Products | 150 | Product categories, brands, and pricing |
| FreshMart_Sales_Transactions | 2,500 | Sales, costs, profit, discounts, and returns |
| FreshMart_Stores | 180 | Store locations, regions, and targets |
| FreshMart_Employees | 350 | Employee and store assignments |
| FreshMart_Suppliers | 85 | Supplier information and ratings |

*Record counts are based on the supplied Business Requirements Document.*

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard is designed to track the following metrics:

- **Total Sales:** Total revenue generated from transactions.
- **Total Cost:** Total cost associated with transactions.
- **Total Profit:** Profit generated from sales.
- **Profit Margin (%):** Profit as a proportion of sales.
- **Total Transactions:** Number of validated sales transactions.
- **Total Units Sold:** Total quantity sold.
- **Average Transaction Value:** Average sales value per transaction.
- **Total Discount:** Discounts applied to transactions.
- **Return Rate:** Proportion of transactions or sales affected by returns, depending on the selected definition.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

The project covers the following business analysis areas:

### 1. Executive Performance Analysis
Monitor overall sales, cost, profit, transaction volume, and units sold.

### 2. Store and Regional Analysis
Compare sales and profit across stores and regions to identify areas requiring management attention.

### 3. Product and Category Analysis
Evaluate product, category, subcategory, and brand performance to understand their contribution to sales and profitability.

### 4. Customer Analysis
Explore customer contribution, membership segments, geographic distribution, and purchasing activity where supported by the data.

### 5. Sales Channel Analysis
Compare sales and profit across channels, payment modes, and other available transaction dimensions.

### 6. Discount and Return Analysis
Examine discount patterns and return-status trends to understand their relationship with profitability.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Understand the business requirements.
2. Inspect the supplied datasets and their relationships.
3. Clean and prepare data in Microsoft Excel.
4. Connect datasets using common identifiers.
5. Calculate KPIs and supporting measures.
6. Analyze business performance across key dimensions.
7. Build an interactive dashboard using charts, PivotTables, and slicers.
8. Validate calculations and summarize business insights.
9. Develop recommendations based on the analysis.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of FreshMart's business performance and helps stakeholders:

- Understand the relationship between sales and profitability.
- Compare store and product performance.
- Identify potential underperforming areas.
- Evaluate discount, return, and channel patterns.
- Make informed decisions using data-driven insights.

## ⚠️ Data Considerations

- KPI calculations should be validated against the source data.
- Missing values should be handled according to their business meaning rather than automatically replaced with zero.
- Transaction counts and return rates depend on validated data definitions.
- Store targets require appropriate alignment with the transaction reporting period.
- Observed patterns do not necessarily establish causation.
